LifePilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android application LifePilot, developed by Rita Skuder, handles information when you use the app.

1. Data stored by LifePilot

LifePilot is primarily a local, on-device planning application. Information that you enter into the app—such as tasks, reminders, calendar items, meal plans, recipes, shopping-list items, pantry information, budgets, income, expenses, recurring payments, preferences and settings—is stored locally on your device.

LifePilot does not require you to create a LifePilot account and does not operate its own cloud account service for this information.

2. Internet access and third-party websites

LifePilot uses Internet access when you choose online functionality, such as searching for a recipe or opening a recipe source. In those cases, your search terms and normal network information needed to load the requested page (for example, your IP address and browser/network request information) may be received by the third-party website or service you choose to access. Those third-party services process information under their own privacy policies.

LifePilot does not use the recipe browser to enable JavaScript, DOM storage, file access or content access inside the embedded browser.

3. Google Play purchases

LifePilot may offer paid or Premium features through Google Play Billing. Payments are processed by Google Play. LifePilot does not receive or store your full payment-card or bank-account details. The app may receive purchase and entitlement information from Google Play that is necessary to determine whether paid features are available to you.

4. Notifications and reminders

If you allow notifications, LifePilot uses notification permission to show reminders and alerts that you configure. Reminder information is processed on your device.

5. Calendar export

When you explicitly choose to export an event to a calendar, LifePilot passes the event details to the calendar application or service selected on your device. LifePilot does not silently upload your calendar data to a LifePilot server.

6. Backup, export and import

Android may include eligible LifePilot application data in device backup, depending on your device, Android settings and backup provider. LifePilot may also allow manual export or import initiated by you. Backup providers and destination applications may process data according to their own privacy policies.

7. Advertising and analytics

The current LifePilot release does not include advertising SDKs or third-party analytics SDKs. LifePilot does not sell personal information.

8. Data sharing

LifePilot does not sell or rent your personal information. Data is shared with a third party only when this is necessary for a feature you explicitly use, such as opening a recipe website, using Google Play Billing, exporting to another application, or using Android/device backup.

9. Data security

LifePilot is designed to keep your planning data on your device. Network connections used by the app are restricted to secure HTTPS connections. The security of device backups and third-party services is governed by the security measures of those providers.

10. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored LifePilot data remains on your device until you delete individual entries, use an available in-app reset/delete function, uninstall the app, or clear LifePilot storage through Android settings. If data has been included in a device backup, retention and deletion of that backup are controlled by your Android/device backup provider.

Because LifePilot does not require a LifePilot account, there is no LifePilot online account to delete.

11. Children

LifePilot is a general productivity and personal-planning application and is not specifically directed to children. The app does not knowingly operate a service intended to collect personal information from children.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when LifePilot features or data practices change. The “Last updated” date above will be changed when an update is published.
